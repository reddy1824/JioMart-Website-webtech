# Security Policy

## Vulnerability Disclosure Policy

We take security seriously at JioMart-Website. If you think you have found a security vulnerability in our application, we encourage you to let us know right away. 

### Reporting a Vulnerability
To report a security issue, please send an email to security@jiomart-website.com. 

### Safeguarding Your Submission
Please be sure to include the following information in your report:
- A detailed description of the issue
- Steps to reproduce the issue
- Any relevant screenshots or code snippets

We will acknowledge your report within 24 hours and keep you informed of our progress. 

### Disclosure Timeline
We make it a priority to address reported vulnerabilities as quickly as possible, in accordance with our internal processes.

We thank you for helping to maintain the security of our application!
